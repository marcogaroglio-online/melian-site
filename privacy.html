Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Melian does not collect any personal data. Everything you record stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We, the developer, cannot see it.

Who we are

Melian is made by Marco Mattia Garoglio, an independent developer in Italy (“we”). This policy covers the Melian app for iPhone and its widgets.

What Melian stores, and where

When you use Melian you may record your baby's name, birth date, sex, gestational age and an optional photo, along with feeds, nappy changes, notes and growth measurements, and the name you choose to show on your entries.

  • On your iPhone. This information is saved on your device so the app, widgets and Live Activity can show it.
  • In your iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, Melian syncs it to the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. It is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to your private iCloud data.
  • With the people you invite. If you invite a partner from Settings, the data is shared with them through iCloud sharing. You can stop sharing at any time.
  • In your backups. Melian writes a daily backup file to Files › On My iPhone › Melian. These files stay on your device unless you move or share them.

What we collect

Nothing. Melian has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We do not sell, rent or share data, because we never receive it.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not include the information you record in Melian.

Reminders, alarms and Siri

Feed reminders and alarms are scheduled on your iPhone. If you use Siri or Shortcuts with Melian, your request is handled by Apple.

Children

Melian is used by parents and caregivers to record information about their baby. It is not directed at children. Because we do not collect data, no information about any child ever reaches us.

Your choices

  • To export your data, use Settings › Export data in the app.
  • To delete everything, delete Melian from your iPhone, then remove its data in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Melian. A partner keeps their access until you stop sharing.

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. If you live in the EU or UK, you still have every right granted by the GDPR, and you can contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. A change that starts collecting data would need your consent in the app first.
