Common questions

How do I invite my partner?

Open Settings › Invite partner and send the invitation with Messages, Mail or any app. Your partner opens it on their iPhone with Melian installed, and both phones show the same data from then on.

The alarm didn't ring. What should I check?

In Melian, make sure Settings › Feed reminders › Remind me to feed and Loud alarm are on. Then check that alarms and notifications are allowed for Melian in iOS Settings. Settings › Test the alarm lets you hear it right away.

Does a reminder log a feed by itself?

No. A reminder only tells you it's time. A feed is logged only when you or your partner tap Feed, in the app, on a widget or on the Live Activity.

Where is my data, and how do I back it up?

On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Melian also saves a daily backup in Files › On My iPhone › Melian, and Settings › Export data creates one whenever you want.

How do I delete everything?

Delete Melian, then go to iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Melian and delete its data.

Are the feeding amounts medical advice?

No. They are indicative ranges from published health guidance, shown with their source. Always follow your midwife, health visitor or doctor.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone that runs iOS 26 or later.
