        01

        Log it in one tap.

        Half asleep, one hand on the bottle. Feed, wet, dirty: one tap each, from the app, a widget, the Lock Screen or Siri. Melian remembers the last amount, so most feeds take a second.

        02

        Loud when it matters.

        After each feed Melian sets the next one. When it's time it rings like an alarm, even on silent, and it never logs a feed on its own. Meanwhile the countdown waits on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

        03

        Two phones, one story.

        Invite your partner and both iPhones see the same day, live. Whoever is up at 3 a.m. knows what happened at midnight without waking anyone.

        04

        Guidance, not judgement.

        Soft rings show the day against indicative ranges for your baby's age and weight, with the source one tap away. A reference, never a score, and never a replacement for your midwife or doctor.

        Philosophy

        Made by a tired parent, for tired parents.

        Melian started as a tool for one family's nights. Every decision since follows the same few rules.

        1. One tap, or it doesn't belong.

          If something takes two steps at 3 a.m., it gets redesigned until it takes one.

        2. Calm by default.

          Soft colours, few words, no streaks and no badges. The app stays quiet until the moment it shouldn't.

        3. Your data stays yours.

          No accounts and no servers of ours. Everything lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

        4. It never decides for you.

          Reminders ring, but they never log a feed by themselves. You stay in charge of what happened.

        5. Guidance with sources.

          Every number comes from published health guidance, and the reference is shown. Indicative, never prescriptive.

        6. Made for two.

          Parenting is shared, so the app is too. Both phones see the same day, in real time.

        Privacy

        We never see your baby's data.

        Melian has no accounts and no servers. Feeds, nappies and growth are stored on your iPhone and in your family's private iCloud, shared only with the partner you invite. We can't read it, sell it or lose it.
